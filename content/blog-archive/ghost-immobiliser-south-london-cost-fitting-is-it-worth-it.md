What Is A Ghost Immobiliser And How Does It Work?

A Ghost Immobiliser is an aftermarket security device that prevents your car from starting unless you enter a unique PIN code using the existing buttons in your car.

That's it. Simple concept. But incredibly effective.

Here's how it works in practice:

You get in your car. Before the engine will start, you need to press a sequence of buttons — could be the steering wheel buttons, window buttons, door lock buttons, or any combination of existing controls on your dashboard.

The sequence is your personal PIN. Only you know it. There's no key fob to clone. No signal to relay. No wiring to bypass. Just a code that exists in your head.

If someone gets into your car without the PIN — even if they have your actual key — the engine won't start. The car is completely immobilised.

No visible device to find and remove. No LED lights. No extra key fobs. Nothing for a thief to detect or disable.

The Ghost connects directly to your vehicle's CAN bus network (the data system that runs your car's electronics). It sits behind the dashboard, completely hidden. Even a professional car thief wouldn't know it's there by looking.

That's why it works. It's invisible, undetectable, and impossible to bypass without the PIN.

Can Thieves Bypass A Ghost Immobiliser?

This is the most searched question about Ghost Immobilisers. And I understand why people ask it — you want to know if it actually works before spending money on it.

Short answer: No. Not with current methods.

Longer answer: Here's why.

Relay attacks don't work: Relay devices boost your key fob signal. But even if thieves unlock and enter your car using relay, the engine won't start without the PIN. The relay device can't replicate button presses in a specific sequence.

Key cloning doesn't work: Thieves can clone your key fob. But even with a cloned key, the engine won't start without the PIN.

OBD port hacking doesn't work: Some thieves plug devices into your car's OBD diagnostic port to reprogram new keys. The Ghost blocks this. Even with a new key programmed, the engine won't start without the PIN.

CAN bus injection (headlight hacking): This is the newest method — thieves access the CAN bus through the headlight wiring to send unlock signals. But even if they get into the car this way, the Ghost still prevents the engine from starting without the PIN.

There is no electronic method to bypass a Ghost. The only way to defeat it would be to physically find the unit behind the dashboard and remove it — which would take hours, specialist knowledge, and would likely trigger other alarms. No thief is spending hours dismantling a dashboard in the middle of the night.

Could technology change in the future? Possibly. But Ghost continuously updates their firmware, and as new threats emerge, the system evolves. For right now, in 2026, a Ghost Immobiliser is the most effective anti-theft device available for keyless cars.

What Are The Disadvantages Of A Ghost Immobiliser?

I'm going to be honest here because every product has downsides. And if I pretend there aren't any, you won't trust anything else I say.

You need to remember the PIN: If you forget your PIN, you can't start your car. There's an emergency code process, but it takes time. Write your PIN down somewhere safe at home. Not in the car.

Service mode for garages: When your car goes in for a service, the mechanic needs to start it. You either give them the PIN (not ideal) or put the Ghost into service mode before dropping it off. It's a 30-second process but you need to remember to do it.

If your insurance requires Thatcham approved security, you need a GPS tracker (MetaTrak S5 or S7) alongside the Ghost. Professional installation required: You can't DIY this. It needs proper CAN bus integration by someone who knows what they're doing. If it's installed wrong, it could cause electrical issues or not work properly. Always use a professional installer.

Those are the real disadvantages. None of them are deal-breakers. Remembering a PIN is a small price for keeping your car safe.
