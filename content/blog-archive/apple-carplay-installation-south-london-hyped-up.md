### Apple CarPlay vs Android Auto: What's Actually Different

People always ask: "Which one should I get?"

**Real answer:** You don't choose. Your phone does.

**How it actually works:**

- iPhone connects? You get Apple CarPlay
- Android phone connects? You get Android Auto
- Both systems in one stereo? Automatic switching
**The differences that matter:**

**Apple CarPlay:**

- Works with any iPhone (5S and up)
- Uses Siri voice control
- Integrates with Apple ecosystem (iMessage, Apple Music, Apple Maps)
- Wireless option available on newer stereos
- Cleaner interface (Apple's thing)
**Android Auto:**

- Works with any Android phone (5.0+)
- Uses Google Assistant
- Deep integration with Google services (Maps, Assistant, Play Music)
- Wired connection standard (wireless on premium units)
- More customizable
**Honest take:** They're basically the same thing now. Use whichever phone you've got. Don't stress about choosing.
