### Ghost Immobiliser: The Invisible Security Nobody Knows Is There

Right, so MetaTrak is great for recovery. But prevention is better than recovery.

**Enter the Ghost immobiliser.**

This is probably my favorite security device. Not because it's fancy or technical (it is, but that's not why I love it) - I love it because it's invisible and it actually works.

#### What Ghost Does (And Why It Matters)

Your car has an engine. Engine won't start without electrical signals from the immobiliser saying "yeah, this is the right key."

Factory immobiliser? Modern, but not amazing. Sophisticated thieves can bypass it.

**Ghost immobiliser?** You can't bypass it without knowing a secret code sequence.

How it works:

1. You press the ignition
2. You enter a secret PIN using your steering wheel buttons (or other controls)
3. Ghost validates the PIN
4. Only then does the car start
No PIN? **Car doesn't start.** Period. Not even if they've got your keys.

It's like a secret handshake between you and your car.

#### Why Ghost Is Genius in London

**Relay attacks** are becoming common. Thief stands outside your house with a device, clones your key signal while you sleep, then drives away in your car next morning.

Ghost stops this completely. Even with a cloned key, the car won't start without the PIN.

**OBD attacks** - thieves plug into your diagnostic port and reprogram the immobiliser.

Ghost works independently of your OBD. Can't reprogram it. Can't bypass it.

**Hot-wiring** - old school but still happens.

Ghost is on the CAN bus network. Even if they physically hot-wire the ignition, Ghost won't allow the fuel pump/starter to engage.

#### Professional Installation Is Non-Negotiable

Ghost is CAN bus based, which means it integrates with your car's existing electrical network. No visible wires, no boxes, no obvious security device.

**Professional installation is absolutely essential** because:

- CAN bus is complex (wrong installation can cause electrical issues)
- Wire routing matters (hidden properly or it's a liability)
- PIN configuration needs to be set up correctly
- Testing is critical (we need to verify it works 100% before you leave)
Bad installation? Could damage your ECU, drain your battery, or worse - the system might not work when you actually need it.

We handle all Ghost installations with full testing and a warranty.
