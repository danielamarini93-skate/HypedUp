### The Three Paths: Air, Coils, or Springs

Right, let's talk options because this isn't a one-size-fits-all situation.

#### **Air Suspension - The Ultimate Solution**

This is where my heart is. Full air ride systems give you everything:

- **Adjustable on the fly** - Press a button, change your height
- **Individual corner control** - Perfect that stance, millimeter by millimeter
- **Memory settings** - Save different heights for different situations
- **Quality of life** - No more three-point turns over speed bumps
- **Show-ready whenever** - Drop it to the floor for photos, raise it back up to leave
**Air Lift Performance** is what we specialize in at Hyped Up. We're authorized dealers for Air Lift and Prazis, and there's a reason for that - the quality is spot on, the support is excellent, and the community around these systems is massive. You'll never be stuck if something needs sorting.

My red Mini's on Air Lift 3P. Four years, zero issues. The 1 Series I'm building? Air Lift all the way.

**Basic Air Lift installation starts at £1,800** - that gets you a proper, clean install with everything you need to get low and back up again.

**Want to go full custom?** We're talking hard pipes instead of airlines, tanks hidden under bumpers, compressors tucked away where nobody sees them, full boot builds that look like art - **that's bespoke pricing** because every car is different. Every vision is different. Some builds we've done have gone well into the thousands because the customer wanted perfection. And that's what we gave them.

#### **Performance Coilovers - The Purist's Choice**

Look, I get it. Some of you want that fixed, dialed-in setup. No air, no complications, just pure geometry.

Quality coilovers give you:

- **Adjustable ride height** - Set it once, sorted
- **Damping adjustment** - Tune your handling
- **Track-ready** - Proper motorsport heritage
- **Lighter than air** - If weight matters to you
- **Cheaper to maintain** - No compressors, no bags
I run BC Racing, KW, and Bilstein setups for customers who want this route. Nothing wrong with it - it's just permanent. You pick your height, you live with it.

Cost: £800-£2,500 depending on the kit and car.

#### **Lowering Springs - The Gateway Drug**

This is where most of us started. Chuck some springs in, looks better, job done.

But here's the thing - I don't really recommend them anymore unless you're on a tight budget. They're better than stock, sure, but:

- You're stuck with whatever drop they give you
- Handling can be weird with stock dampers
- Ride quality often suffers
- Can't adjust for different situations
Cost: £150-£400 plus fitting.

If you're going springs, at least pair them with decent shocks. Your back will thank you.

## Common Questions (Because Everyone Asks)

**"Will air handle as well as coilovers?"**

Modern Air Lift systems? Yeah, absolutely. The gap has closed massively.

For daily driving and spirited road use, you won't notice a difference. The red Mini handles brilliantly on air.

**"What about reliability?"**

Looked after properly, Air Lift systems are rock solid. My Mini's had the same setup for 4 years, no issues. You need to:

- Drain the tank every few months (condensation)
- Check fittings annually
- Keep the compressor area clean
- Use quality components
Buy cheap, expect problems. Buy quality Air Lift through authorized dealers, expect years of service.

**"Can I install it myself?"**

*Technically* yes. *Realistically?* Unless you've got proper fabrication skills, suspension knowledge, and a fully kitted workshop, probably not.

There's a reason we're authorized dealers - we know these systems inside out. The kit might save you money DIY, but fixing a bad install costs more than having it done right first time.

**"How low can you actually go?"**

Depends on your car, your wheels, your arches. But properly done? Center of wheel touching the arch, no rubbing, no drama.

My Mini goes silly low. The 1 Series will go lower. It's all about the setup.

**"What about the MOT?"**

As long as everything's properly fitted, secure, and functional, you're fine. I've never had issues. The tester might ask about it, but if it's quality work, there's no problem.
