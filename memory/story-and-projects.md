# Hypa's story and signature projects (for storytelling)

_14 Nov 2026 · Told by Dani. Use for About, homepage, Builds/Projects and social. Hypa gives the final OK on wording._

## The story
- Hypa has been modifying cars since the **early 2000s**, starting with his **first car, a Vauxhall Astra**.
- About **10 years ago he went to work at McLaren**: his passion became his job.
- In **2022 he opened Hyped Up** at Unit 22, Bolina Road, Bermondsey (SE16).
- What makes him different: **passion and quality**. Any car, expensive or not: he finds a solution and modifies it the way
  the customer wants, so they're happy with what they have. **Anything can happen at Hyped Up: no limits** on what he can do.

## Signature projects
| Project | What Hypa did | Why it matters (the story) |
|---|---|---|
| **Audi A4: the silent performer** | Stripped the whole car (even the dashboard), soundproofed everything: cabin, wheel arches, bonnet. Fitted **air suspension with the tank and compressor mounted outside the cabin** so the system makes no noise inside. | The customer wanted Audi power but a quiet car for family and travel, not "just luxury" like a Mercedes. Bespoke engineering for a real need. |
| **Van with roof beacons** (a friend's van) | Beacons fitted on the roof | Work and safety builds, not just show cars (see Instagram posts) |
| **The red Mini** (Hyped Up's own, F56 JCW) | A big project, now finished: Air Lift 3P, **boot rebuilt 3 times, a new boot build each season** | The workshop's flagship; daily driven on air 4+ years |
| **BMW 1 Series convertible** (Hyped Up's own, next project) | Custom sound system built into the boot (done); next: **wrap and bodykit** | A build in progress to follow |
| **Charity barber's motorbike** (a client and friend who cuts hair for homeless people for charity) | **Custom Bluetooth speakers built into the rear box** of his motorbike | Proof that if you can imagine it, Hypa makes it happen, and the human side of the brand |
