# Brand Voice & Writing Rules

_Last updated: 7 Nov 2026_

## Hypa's voice
- First person, direct, conversational
- Honest about pros **and** cons of products
- Anti-corporate — "no corporate fluff"
- South London authentic; reference real places (Bermondsey, Peckham, Rotherhithe, Camberwell, Old Kent Road, Elephant and Castle)
- Passionate about cars; uses real workshop stories and customer examples
- References personal builds (red F56 Mini on Air Lift, 1 Series show build, old R53 "Lil Punisher")
- Phrases he uses: "here's the thing", "real talk", "let me be straight"
- Always gives practical advice

## Blog post format
- Titles include a location keyword (South London, Bermondsey) where natural
- Meta title ≤ 65 characters; meta description 155–160 characters
- Bullet lists for scanability
- FAQ section built from real searched questions (AnswerThePublic / Search Console), formatted for FAQ schema
- Cross-sell other services naturally; cross-link to other posts
- **Every post ends with:** contact details, pricing, and a P.S.

## Competitor mention rules
- ✅ Name product/manufacturer brands: Ghost, Pandora, Scorpion, MetaTrak, Xtrons, Autostyle, Pioneer, Kenwood, Rockford Fosgate, Air Lift, Prazis
- ✅ Compare product brands honestly (e.g. Ghost vs Pandora vs Scorpion)
- ✅ Generic references: "other shops", "dodgy tuners", "cheap installers"
- ❌ **Never** name a competing installation shop

## Factual guardrails
- **Bespoke, always:** every job is made to order; say "I listen first" and "made for your car", never imply off-the-shelf work (Dani, 14 Nov 2026)
- **Only number plates are "while you wait"**; never promise same-day or in-a-day for anything else
- No dyno testing — never mention it
- Full wraps: minimum 1 week
- Prices only from `pricing.md`

## Website tone of voice v2 (Dani, 18 Nov 2026), applies to everything
Dani: "Fitted properly, priced clearly" and similar lines are too flat. The voice should be **conversational, easy to
understand, modification-focused, direct, assertive, SEO-friendly and knowledge-heavy**: we know what we're talking about,
we're not improvising, and it doesn't read like AI wrote it.

**Reference vibe:** UK car culture (Gravity, Caffeine & Machine, TRAX). What they do, read from their sites on 18 Nov:
- Talk to the reader as one of the scene: "Think your build has what it takes?", "Not in a car club? No worries!"
- Use the culture's own words with confidence: build, spec list, show and shine, track build, street build, air ride, stance, mod.
- Wide and inclusive: "From first cars to hypercars, spotless classics to superbikes, microcars to trucks."
- Short, assertive claims, no hedging: "This isn't just another show and shine." "All machines are always welcome."
- Craft words: "creativity, craftsmanship, and modification done right", "attention to detail".

**Rules for Hyped Up copy**
1. **Lead with the mod, not the business.** Say what goes on the car and what it does ("no code, no start, even with your key").
2. **Prove you know it.** One real technical detail per block: brand, part, method or trade-off (Air Lift 3P, Prazis struts,
   tank and compressor placement, wiring loomed and hidden, prep before film). Only facts from `memory/` or Hypa; never invent specs.
3. **Short sentences. Plain words.** Explain jargon in five words or fewer when a first-timer might not know it.
4. **Assertive, not salesy.** State it; no "we strive to", "high-quality solutions", "one-stop shop", "fitted properly, priced clearly".
5. **Talk to "you", speak as "we"** (the workshop). Use "I" only for Hypa's own story and quotes (About, builds he narrates). _TBC Dani._
6. **Inclusive range:** any car or bike, cheap or expensive. Name real examples (first cars, vans, R6s, supercars).
7. **SEO without stuffing:** the service + place keyword once in the H1/lead and once in body ("car wrapping in Bermondsey"), then write for people.
8. **Honest trade-offs** build trust: say when something isn't worth it, or what it can't do.
9. **CTAs are a nudge, not a form label:** "What are we building?", "Show us the car", "Send the spec".

**Before → after**
- "South London's one-stop workshop for car and bike security, upgrades and styling. Fitted properly, priced clearly."
  → "Security, screens, audio, wraps and air ride for cars and bikes. Built in Bermondsey by people who mod their own."
- "Your car won't start without your code." → "Ghost immobiliser: no code, no start, even with your key."
- "Colour changes, designed with you, fitted by hand." → "Colour changes, camo, stripes, logos. A full wrap takes at least a week, because the prep is the job."
