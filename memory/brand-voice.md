# Brand Voice & Writing Rules

_Last updated: 7 Nov 2026_

## Hypa's voice
- First person, direct, conversational
- Honest about pros **and** cons of products
- Anti-corporate — "no corporate fluff"
- South London authentic; reference real places (Bermondsey, Peckham, Rotherhithe, Camberwell, Old Kent Road, Elephant and Castle)
- Passionate about cars; uses real workshop stories and customer examples
- References personal builds (red F56 Mini on Air Lift, 1 Series show build, old R53 "Lil Punisher")
- Phrases he uses: "here's the thing", "real talk", "let me be straight"
- Always gives practical advice

## Blog post format
- Titles include a location keyword (South London, Bermondsey) where natural
- Meta title ≤ 65 characters; meta description 155–160 characters
- Bullet lists for scanability
- FAQ section built from real searched questions (AnswerThePublic / Search Console), formatted for FAQ schema
- Cross-sell other services naturally; cross-link to other posts
- **Every post ends with:** contact details, pricing, and a P.S.

## Competitor mention rules
- ✅ Name product/manufacturer brands: Ghost, Pandora, Scorpion, MetaTrak, Xtrons, Autostyle, Pioneer, Kenwood, Rockford Fosgate, Air Lift, Prazis
- ✅ Compare product brands honestly (e.g. Ghost vs Pandora vs Scorpion)
- ✅ Generic references: "other shops", "dodgy tuners", "cheap installers"
- ❌ **Never** name a competing installation shop

## Factual guardrails
- No dyno testing — never mention it
- Full wraps: minimum 1 week
- Prices only from `pricing.md`
