## What it says
1. **Google Maps is Hyped Up's main front door.** ~145 interactions a month, against ~19 website visitors a week. Many customers decide from the Maps listing alone.
2. **People come to the unit:** 400 direction requests. The new site needs a strong "visit" block: map link, what the unit looks like (mural), parking, hours.
3. **Calls are the biggest measurable enquiry:** ~18/month from Maps vs 1 website form submission in 3 months. Phone must stay one tap away on mobile, and calls should be tracked on the site too.
4. **The listing sends ~41 visits/month to the website**: the page it links to must load fast and answer "what, how much, where" straight away.
5. **Bookings = 0** because no booking link is set. Option: point Google's booking/"Get a quote" button at the WhatsApp quote link.
