## What the data says
1. **People who find Hyped Up mostly already know the name.** Of the clicks Google attributes to a query, 57 of 83 are brand searches ("hyped up limited", "hyped up"). Non-brand searches got **5,692 impressions but only 26 clicks**, because the pages sit on pages 2–6 of Google.
2. **The demand is there, the rankings aren't.** Biggest non-brand searches we already appear for (impressions, position):
   - Remapping: "car remapping london" 175 (pos 24), "remapping london" 161 (20), "ecu remapping london" 132 (22), "car remap london" 102 (30)
   - Security: "car security london" 159 (25), "vehicle cctv london" 82 (39), "car tracker london" 70 (41), "immobilisers greater london" 66 (23)
   - Audio and CarPlay: "sound system installation" 159 (52), "car audio upgrade" 94 (39), "android car play greater london" 78 (17), "apple car play greater london" 67 (33)
   - **Local area searches** for audio upgrades: New Addington, Purley, Sidcup, Orpington, Chislehurst, Caterham (58–79 each, pos 10–35) → South-East London area mentions are worth having on service pages.
3. **Pages with the most untapped visibility:** Audio (2,502 impressions, pos 29, 0.3% CTR) · Remapping (1,465, pos 24) · Multimedia (910, pos 33) · Security (896, pos 30). These become dedicated, answer-shaped service pages.
4. **Phones do much better:** mobile CTR 3.64% at pos 9.5 vs desktop 1.13% at pos 27. Mobile-first is right.
5. **Duplicate addresses split the ranking:** the homepage is indexed as both `www.hypedup.uk` (116 clicks) and `hypedup.uk` (59). Blog posts appear under **three** patterns: `/hyped-up/f/…`, `/f/…` and `/blog/f/…`. The redirect plan must cover all of them, and the new site must use one address per page.
6. **The DVLA post is a hidden asset:** "The Legal Reality of Car Mods in the UK" has 730 impressions at **position 8.7**. Fix its facts and keep it, rather than drop it.
7. **Clothing searches exist** ("hyped up clothing"): the apparel page is worth keeping.

## Targets to track after launch (proposal)
- Non-brand clicks: from ~9/month (26 in 3 months) to **50+/month** within 6 months
- Average position for the four service pages: from 24–33 to **top 10**
- Total clicks: from ~78/month to **200+/month** within 6 months
