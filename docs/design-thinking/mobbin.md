# Mobbin research (web sections)

_Date: 2026-11-08 · Platform: web · Saved to the private Mobbin collection
[Hyped Up website redesign](https://mobbin.com/collections/c6d3c576-1851-462e-bd0e-4df750e1020e/mobile/screens)
(only Dani and teammates can open it). High-res copies: `mobbin/`. Mobbin has almost no car-workshop
sites, so these are **patterns to borrow** from creative studios, mobility brands, local venues and merch shops._

## 1. Hero: personality + a real thing
- [Freshman: handwritten scribble on red](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/af17ed24-6080-40d0-8320-b404bc9025bb) (`hero-freshman-scribble.jpg`): a loose hand-drawn line and handwritten note ("white balance 4,500K") on flat brand red. **Borrow:** a hand-drawn layer (tags, arrows, notes) on brand red, as a signature graphic language.
- [Freshman: car video + handwritten annotations](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/efd2c199-fe77-4b3a-971a-0ec6bdeb686b) (`hero-freshman-annotated.jpg`): bold caps headline over car footage, a word struck through in red, a hand-circled "explore all work" link. **Borrow:** this is the closest match to Hyped Up: automotive + graffiti-style marks on top of clean type.
- [Lightship: giant type over photo](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/82617eb6-17e0-48de-9482-e73470a08d1f) (`hero-lightship-bigtype.jpg`): one huge word over an aerial vehicle shot. **Borrow:** scale and confidence. **Avoid:** text over busy photos without a scrim (contrast).
- Also seen: [Rivian](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2ad00f6a-e535-4ebf-b78f-3cdc97174ab8) and [VanMoof](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/797e281b-ffe2-4d8e-adce-5e5515e54b1b): full-bleed film with a single action at the bottom. Beautiful but heavy; use a still + short optional video on mobile.

## 2. Services: photo cards beat icon cards
- [YLLW: photo cards with condensed caps](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/4992ac8d-926b-404b-8042-8f1ff17fd7a4) (`services-yllw-photo-cards.jpg`): each service is a real workshop photo with a bold uppercase title and one line. **Borrow:** exactly this for electronics and styling services, using Hyped Up's own photos.
- [Phantom Studios: service rows](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3cf723db-92c1-4601-b820-379757d44107) (`services-phantom-rows.jpg`): big title, short description, "view work" link, thumbnail per row. **Borrow:** for a two-pillar layout (Electronics / Styling) with services listed under each.
- [Fiasco: colour-block cards](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/814e0a13-81d8-4d89-95f1-33b72ef0d9d3) (`services-fiasco-cards.jpg`): two big playful cards. **Borrow:** two pillars as two big doors on mobile ("Electronics" / "Style it").
- **Avoid:** generic icon grids (emoji/illustration cards); they're what the current site and competitors do.

## 3. Work / gallery: let the builds talk
- [Vucko: horizontal rail with captions](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ceab4ab4-d046-4356-bde4-32acd9781fa8) (`gallery-vucko-rail.jpg`): large cards scroll sideways, each with a two-line caption (project + client). **Borrow:** "Build + what we did" captions (e.g. "Abarth 595 · Hexis pink wrap + CarPlay"). Swipe-friendly on phones.
- [Studio Freight: dark wall of work](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/e17cb69f-b5cd-4aa4-90ca-82838a400641) (`gallery-studiofreight-wall.jpg`): a dense, mixed-size collage on black, "drag to explore". **Borrow:** the collage energy (like a workshop wall of photos and stickers) for the Work page, not the homepage.

## 4. Reviews: Google, real names, short quotes
- [Analogue Agency: Google reviews row](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/180dbf78-b891-48df-a7d7-c0e5265481bc) (`reviews-analogue-google.jpg`): Google logo, 5 stars, short quotes, first name + "a week ago", "All reviews →". **Borrow:** this, fed from Hyped Up's Google Business Profile (with permission).
- [Framer: masonry of review cards](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b6b31012-bb6b-4b3d-8016-efc66b42a87a) (`reviews-framer-masonry.jpg`): lots of short reviews in a dark masonry. **Borrow:** volume as proof, on a Reviews page.

## 5. Bold / graffiti energy
- [Slush: sticker-style type tiles](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/bef161b2-213e-47d0-98e1-7769e041eb86) (`bold-slush-stickers.jpg`): condensed italic caps in coloured tiles with 3D stickers. **Borrow:** a sticker-bomb strip (like stickers on a toolbox or bumper) as a section divider or marquee.
- [Wild: pixel marquee](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b7197fb0-f4c9-4583-8864-d0f5e1c94195) (`bold-wild-marquee.jpg`): a playful scrolling statement before the contact CTA. **Borrow:** a moving tagline strip ("WE ADAPT THE CAR TO YOU →") above the final CTA. Respect reduced-motion.

## 6. Visit the workshop
- [Monte: contact / hours / find us on brand colour](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6ecba422-fe26-482b-8225-8e24ec959d84) (`visit-monte.jpg`): three clear columns on a brand-colour block, plus a hand-drawn illustration and "I need a real map →". **Borrow:** the warmth and clarity; red block, hours, address, WhatsApp.
- [Little Amps Coffee: place photo + hours](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/74a3a0da-d93a-419b-a675-1a641b46a4eb) (`visit-littleamps.jpg`): address, hours and a short story beside a photo carousel of the shop. **Borrow:** show the unit and the mural so people recognise it when they arrive.
- [Ragged Edge: contact list with copy buttons](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5f1d322f-b76b-4468-90ef-ab85700b0c44) (`visit-raggededge.jpg`): simple rows (find us, call us, email us) with "copy". **Borrow:** tap-to-copy address and phone on mobile.

## 7. Merch (request on WhatsApp)
- [Telepathic Instruments: lifestyle merch rail](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/92682247-5cb5-460a-bc00-b7cc12b48606) (`merch-telepathic.jpg`): products photographed in context, with an announcement marquee. **Borrow:** shoot merch in the workshop/on the cars.
- [Phantom: tinted product cards](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/151e0888-a1ff-4e13-8901-294af81fa94b) (`merch-phantom.jpg`): product on brand-tinted cards, name + price. **Borrow:** card layout with a "Request on WhatsApp" button instead of cart.
- [BitcoinOS: dark merch grid](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/9f8a47e9-de3c-4469-9fff-09b24b009d1e) (`merch-bitcoinos.jpg`): dark cards, category filter in big type. Fine for later, if the range grows.

## Takeaways for the homepage concept
1. **Clean structure, wild surface.** Bold condensed type and a strict grid, with a hand-drawn/graffiti layer (circles, arrows, tags, stickers) on top. Freshman shows it works with cars.
2. **Real photos as the main visual.** Services and work in Hyped Up's own photos (YLLW, Vucko), never icons or stock.
3. **Two pillars as two doors:** Electronics · Styling.
4. **Google reviews row** high on the page.
5. **Moving tagline strip** before the final CTA; visit block on brand red.
6. **Watch-outs:** text over photos needs a scrim; motion must respect reduced-motion; video only on demand on mobile.
