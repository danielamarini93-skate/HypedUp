# Design thinking — intake answers (Dani, 8 Nov 2026)

1. **What:** full redesign of hypedup.uk, built fresh (friend's concept = reference only).
   Apparel shop stays, but simpler: "request on WhatsApp" instead of PayPal/checkout.
2. **Why redesign (Dani's words, summarised):**
   - More **flexibility and creativity** than GoDaddy templates/builder
   - More **SEO- and AI-oriented** (rank in Google, get cited by AI assistants)
   - **Reach more customers**
   - **Run everything from Claude** without opening a browser (publish, update, report)
   - But keep control of **all data and analytics**, including **data from emails**, with an
     **easy dashboard** to analyse the website and its performance
   - So the strategy merges **design/UX** and **technical** decisions
3. **Focus / positioning:** the main push is **electronics and electronic modifications**:
   trackers, dash cams, upgraded CarPlay screens, custom audio. Then **aesthetics/styling**:
   wraps, carbon interiors. "Anything the client wants, we adapt the car to their needs and style."
4. **Assets available:** lots of **new photos and videos**; lots of content in **Google Maps
   reviews and updates** (Google Business Profile).
5. **Research picks:** not answered, proposed in plan.md (to confirm).
6. **Depth:** medium.
7. **Concept focus:** homepage (mobile first).
8. **Visual direction:** reflect the **workshop**. Hypa is a **very creative and happy** person.
   Follow the **brand colours** (red / black / white from the logo), add **graffiti style,
   art and craft**, with **automotive and motorbike spirit**.
