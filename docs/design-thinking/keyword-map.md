volume). **Rule: one primary term per page; no two pages chase the same primary.** Hubs take the broad "group" term; service pages
take the specific term; car-specific pages take "service + make". **No area pages** (Dani, 9 Nov): towns are covered by
`areaServed` in the LocalBusiness schema, an "Areas we cover" line on Contact, and natural mentions in service copy._

## Cannibalisation checks (resolved)
- "car security london" → **Security hub only**; service pages use their product term.
- "immobiliser" → **Ghost page** owns it (Metablok/Scorpion are sections there), not the hub.
- "paint protection" → **PPF page** (Styling), never the Security hub.
- "remap" variants → **one Remapping page**; bikes get a section, not a page.
- Blog posts target **questions** ("is a Ghost worth it", "car wrap cost uk") and link to the service page that owns the primary term.
