# Hyped Up: Project brief

_Version: 0.1 · Date: 2026-11-08 · Owner: Dani_

## 1. Project summary
Hyped Up is a vehicle electronics, security and styling workshop in Bermondsey, South London,
run by Hypa: creative, upbeat, with a McLaren motorsport background. The current website is a
GoDaddy template. It looks generic, holds the brand back and limits SEO. We're redesigning it
from scratch so it **looks and feels like the workshop** (graffiti, art and craft, car and
motorbike culture), **wins more local customers** through Google and AI assistants, and can be
**run entirely from Claude**, while Hyped Up **owns all its data**. Now, because the content
engine (15 posts, 2 a week) and Google Maps ads are bringing traffic to a site that can't keep up.

## 3. Audiences
- **Primary: local drivers wanting electronics and security** (South/South East London).
  Tasks: understand the options (Ghost, tracker, dash cam, CarPlay), see a rough price, trust
  the workshop, ask for a quote fast, usually on a phone and often via WhatsApp.
- **Primary: enthusiasts and stylists** (cars and motorbikes). Tasks: see real work, get
  inspired, check the workshop "gets it", talk to Hypa about a custom build (wrap, carbon
  interior, air, audio, boot build).
- **Secondary: quick-job customers** (plates, tints, dash cam fit). Tasks: price, same-day?,
  directions, opening hours.
- **Secondary: fleet, trade and taxi drivers** (trackers, CCTV, signage). Tasks: capability, multi-vehicle quote. TBC how important.
- **Secondary: fans and community.** Follow the builds, buy apparel.
- **Also: search engines and AI assistants** as "readers" that must understand services, area, prices and proof.

## 4. Scope
- **In:**
  - New visual identity on the web (built on the existing logo and colours) + design system in Claude Design
  - Information architecture: home, a page per service (electronics first), work/gallery, about Hypa and the workshop, journal (blog, all 15+ posts migrated), apparel (request via WhatsApp), contact/visit, prices
  - Quote journey: one main action (WhatsApp with a pre-written message), plus call and email
  - SEO and AI search foundations: structured data, page per service, FAQs, local signals, 301 redirects from every old URL
  - Data and analytics: tracking of enquiry clicks, Search Console, a simple performance dashboard, email data (TBC)
  - Platform choice and migration off GoDaddy hosting (domain and Microsoft 365 email stay put)
  - A way to update content and publish from Claude
- **Out (for now):** online checkout and payments, customer accounts, online booking calendar,
  paid ads redesign, new photography direction (we use Dani's new photos/videos), Google Business Profile overhaul (separate task).

## 5. Constraints
- **Budget:** TBC (aim: free or low-cost hosting and tools)
- **Timeline:** TBC. Ideally before spring show season; seasonal posts continue meanwhile
- **Platform/tech:** domain stays at GoDaddy; email is Microsoft 365 via GoDaddy and must not break; must be editable from Claude; no data loss (GA4 `G-PZPFNLV5SW`, Search Console)
- **Brand:** existing logo (red/black/white); graffiti and craft feel must stay legible and accessible (WCAG 2.2 AA)
- **Legal/claims:** no unsourced claims; tint limits, plate rules and "tell your insurer" where relevant. Content-rule clashes are still to be decided with Hypa (see `memory/friend-concept.md`)
- **People:** Dani designs in spare time; Hypa signs off facts and prices

## 6. Success metrics
- Enquiry clicks per month (by service and channel). Check monthly from launch, compare with a pre-launch baseline
- Non-brand Search Console clicks and impressions. Check monthly, judge at 3 and 6 months
- Core Web Vitals "good" on mobile; accessibility checks pass. Check at launch
- AI assistant visibility test. Monthly
- Every old URL redirects (no traffic drop in the month after launch)
