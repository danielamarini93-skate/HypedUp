## What this means for the IA
1. **Service pages are the real front doors** (search, AI, partners land there): each must work alone: price, proof, FAQ, one action.
2. **Contact is the end of every path** and must carry the service from the page they came from.
3. **Hub pages are for browsing and SEO**, not the main path: reached from the footer, breadcrumbs and in-page links.
4. **Car-specific pages** answer tasks 3–4 directly; start with CarPlay (BMW, Mini, Mercedes, Audi) and Car audio (BMW).
5. **Status + map + phone** must be one tap away everywhere (task 1 is the most frequent real action).
6. **Builds** is the Instagram landing page: fast, visual, filterable, every build linking to its service.
