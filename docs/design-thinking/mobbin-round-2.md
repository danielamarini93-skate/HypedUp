# Mobbin research, round 2: menus and footers

_8 Nov 2026 · Web · Saved to the private collection "Hyped Up website redesign" · Images in `mobbin/` (nav-*, footer-*).
Inner-page patterns (service, gallery, blog) are researched at step 7._

## Mobile menu
- [Open: full-screen black menu](https://mobbin.com/screens/a1869a59-77fb-4ff5-8149-527f1fb06747) (`nav-open-fullscreen.jpg`): five huge words with arrows, small secondary links in a row at the bottom. **Borrow:** our mobile menu = **Protect → Upgrade → Style →** in big condensed type, then Motorbikes · Business · Builds · Blog small, WhatsApp/call fixed at the bottom. Minimal and on-brand.
- [ZARA: big categories + sub-list + images](https://mobbin.com/screens/fab9fd81-4397-423b-a03a-0c5c778c92fa) (`nav-zara-mega.jpg`): category words on the left, the chosen category's links in the middle, three images on the right. **Borrow:** desktop panel for each verb: services listed, one or two build photos for the picked group.

## Desktop dropdown / mega-menu
- [Skillshare: categories + "by type" + Explore](https://mobbin.com/screens/bb577de1-ba8a-496e-9618-424953bd9ee1) (`nav-skillshare-mega.jpg`): main list left, a second column of grouped shortcuts, and a "Blog" link. **Borrow:** a small right column in each panel: "Not sure? Send us your idea" + related guide from the blog.
- [Beside: simple industry dropdown](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c7ef469a-1eec-485d-9a0f-d61416ab7d4f) (`nav-beside-dropdown.jpg`): a short list ending with "All industries". **Borrow:** each panel ends with "All Protect services →" (the hub, where tier-2 pages live).
- **Avoid:** SaaS-style menus with 5+ dropdowns (ClickUp, Ramp): too heavy for a 3-group service business.

## Footer
- [Touchy Coffee: hand-drawn circles around info](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1262461a-ee8b-472e-9cf1-001b0de70548) (`footer-touchy-handdrawn.jpg`): location and contact circled in marker, spaced letter labels, a script wordmark. **Borrow:** the hand-drawn marker circles around "Find us" and hours: matches our workshop-wall marks.
- [Klarna: giant wordmark under the columns](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b206c431-554f-4a83-8660-60cedfa11c15) (`footer-klarna-wordmark.jpg`) and [Runway: outlined giant wordmark](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/902d3be1-1cae-4cf3-bb62-ea51a2008af7) (`footer-runway-outline.jpg`): **Borrow:** a huge "HYPED UP" (or "SE16") outline wordmark as the footer's base: confident, cheap to render.
- [Loom: clear link columns](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/57c75a33-b3df-4a8e-923e-685a4214b258) (`footer-loom-columns.jpg`): 6 columns of plain links. **Borrow:** the place for **tier-2 pages** (all services listed by group) without crowding the menu.
- [Monte: contact / hours / find us](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6ecba422-fe26-482b-8225-8e24ec959d84) (saved in round 1): three bold columns on brand colour.

## Takeaways for our menu and footer
1. **Mobile menu:** full-screen, three big verbs, small secondary links, WhatsApp + call always at the bottom.
2. **Desktop:** logo · Protect · Upgrade · Style · Builds · Visit + one "Get a quote" button. Panels list tier-1 pages and end with "All … services →".
3. **Footer:** all services by group (tier 2 lives here) · Find us + hours (hand-drawn circle) · Company links · socials · giant outline wordmark · legal row.
