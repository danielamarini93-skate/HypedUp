# Proto-personas

_Date: 2026-11-08 · **Proto-personas: not based on interviews.** Built from keyword data,
Search Console, the customer tracker (plates), blog topics, competitor review themes and
Dani's/Hypa's knowledge of customers. Validate before relying on them (see "To check")._

---

## 1. Kemi: the worried owner
**"I just want to know my car will still be there in the morning."**

- **Who:** 30s–50s, lives in SE London (Peckham, Bermondsey, Lewisham), drives a car that's on the most-stolen lists (Range Rover, Golf, Fiesta, BMW). Heard about keyless theft from a neighbour or the news.
- **Trigger:** a theft on her street, an insurer asking for a tracker, buying a new car.
- **Searches:** "ghost immobiliser near me", "car tracker uk", "is ghost worth it", "most stolen cars london".
- **Needs:** understand Ghost vs tracker vs dash cam in plain words; a price; reassurance the fitter is legit; fit soon.
- **Fears:** being upsold; a fitter damaging the car's electrics; paying for something that doesn't work.
- **On the site:** lands on a service page from Google on her phone, scans for price and "how it works", checks reviews, then WhatsApps.
- **What wins her:** calm, clear security pages (even inside a graffiti brand), real prices, Google reviews, a face (Hypa), brands she recognises (Ghost, MetaTrak).
- **Services:** Ghost, trackers, dash cams, security packages.

## 2. Jay: the tech upgrader
**"My car's fine, but the screen's from 2014."**

- **Who:** 20s–40s, commutes or drives for work (incl. taxi and delivery drivers), owns an older premium car (BMW, Mercedes, Audi, Mini) or a work vehicle.
- **Trigger:** phone mount fatigue, wants wireless CarPlay, reversing camera, better sound, or a dash cam for work protection.
- **Searches:** "apple carplay retrofit", "front and rear dash cam", "car audio upgrade london", "carplay for bmw".
- **Needs:** "will it work on *my* car?", a price, keeps steering wheel controls, looks factory.
- **Fears:** cheap screen that lags, wiring mess, warranty problems.
- **On the site:** wants to pick his car model or send his reg; compares options; WhatsApps a photo of his dash.
- **What wins him:** car-specific examples ("CarPlay in a Mini F56"), before/after photos, partner brands (Xtrons, Autostyle, Pioneer, Kenwood), "from £" price.
- **Services:** CarPlay/Android Auto, cameras, sensors, dash cams, audio.

## 3. Marco: the enthusiast and stylist
**"I want it to look like nobody else's."**

- **Who:** 18–35, car or motorbike enthusiast, active on Instagram/TikTok, goes to meets and shows. Mini, Golf, 1 Series, Abarth, Civic, a naked bike or super sport.
- **Trigger:** a new build, a show coming up, saw a wrap or a bagged car on Instagram.
- **Searches:** "car wrap cost uk", "air suspension installation near me", "chrome delete", "custom boot build", or comes straight from Instagram.
- **Needs:** inspiration, proof the workshop "gets it", to talk ideas through with the person doing the work, a rough budget.
- **Fears:** a shop that doesn't care about details; a rushed wrap; being treated like just another job.
- **On the site:** scrolls the work, watches videos, checks Hypa's own builds (red Mini on Air Lift, the 1 Series), maybe buys a tee, then DMs or WhatsApps.
- **What wins him:** the graffiti/art energy, real builds, Hypa's story and cars, community (meets, shows), merch.
- **Services:** wraps (full, partial, chameleon, chrome delete), carbon interiors, air suspension, audio/boot builds, plates, tint, remaps.

## Secondary
- **Quick-job customer:** needs plates, tint or a dash cam fitted *today*; wants price, hours, directions. Serve with a clear "quick jobs" strip and the visit block.
- **Fleet / trade:** trackers, CCTV for taxis, van signage. A short "for business" line + contact is enough for now (importance TBC).

## What this means for the homepage
- Two doors: **Electronics** (Kemi, Jay) and **Styling** (Marco).
- Proof for everyone: real work + Google reviews + Hypa.
- Security content stays calm and clear inside the creative brand (Kemi's trust).
- Car-specific hooks for Jay ("Got a BMW, Mini, Mercedes? See what fits").
- Culture and merch for Marco.
