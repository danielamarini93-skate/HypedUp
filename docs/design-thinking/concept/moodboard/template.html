Design thinking · Round 2 · Visual direction · v2

Style moodboard

Three ways to bring graffiti into Hyped Up, built from Dani's references, plus D: the best of all three. Each band: the references, a larger Hyped Up mock in that style (A and C switch between light and dark), then palette, type and a short read.

The rule all references follow: big, clean type carries the message; graffiti is one real layer on top, a tag or a spray word set in a proper font, or real graffiti in the photo. No fake spray.

Direction A

Street editorial

A car-culture magazine. Wide heavy type, mono captions, black-and-white halftone photos with one orange tag, stickers, crop marks and a barcode. Photo: the red Mini at a show, someone in a hoodie beside it: culture and people.

Direction D · new

Workshop wall

The best of A, B and C in one. B's dark base, condensed type, spray word and red → orange gradient; A's editorial frame (meta line, numbered columns, crop marks) and colour media with a strong vignette; C's calm split layout, square media and the hand-drawn circle. The hero is the real workshop reel, as Dani asked for video above the fold.
