What changed in v3

  • Real paint marks (public-domain SVGs from Openclipart) recoloured in red and orange: splat, drips, spray speckle, brush line, marker circle
  • Graffiti fonts: Hoax Vandal for tags ("Find us", "Not sure…"), Rubik Spray Paint for the HYPED UP wordmark (both free for commercial use)
  • Lucide icons for all UI icons; brand logos (WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook) from Simple Icons, because Lucide has no brand logos
  • Subtle button gradient; bigger logo
  • Toggle at the top: sharp or rounded image corners

Why Motorbikes and Business, but no "Cars"?

Every service page is already about cars, so cars don't need their own page: they're the default. Motorbikes and Business & fleet get hubs because those customers need a different mix (bike wraps, Datatool trackers, bike remaps; fleet trackers, taxi cameras, van signage) and they search for it differently.

Approved

  • 10 services in the menu
  • Blog in the footer only
  • Wordmark: HYPED UP
