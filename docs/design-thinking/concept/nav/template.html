What changed in v4

  • Direction D, dark only: Big Shoulders + Barlow + Plex Mono labels
  • Red → orange gradient on the quote button, open-menu underline, numbers and arrows
  • No paint splats, drips or brush lines: the hand-drawn circle on "Find us" is the only mark
  • Graffiti City once: "Style it." in the phone hero. Footer wordmark: HYPED UP in Big Shoulders, outline only, gradient stroke
  • Square images with the D vignette and mono captions; logo on a dark chip so it works in light mode

Unchanged (approved)

  • 10 services in the menu, grouped Protect · Upgrade · Style
  • Builds and Contact as plain links; Blog in the footer only
  • Footer: every service, find us, hours, socials; no prices, no review widget
  • Wordmark: HYPED UP

Decided (Dani, 9 Nov)

  • Dark only: no light mode on the site
  • No prices in the menu (too prominent): "from £" lives on service pages
