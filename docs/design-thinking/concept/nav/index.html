Why three doors

They mirror the headline "Protect it. Upgrade it. Style it." and match how each persona thinks: Kemi protects, Jay upgrades, Marco styles.

What's in the menu

  • Protect: Ghost & immobilisers, Car trackers, Dash cams, PPF
  • Upgrade: CarPlay, Car audio, Remapping
  • Style: Car wrapping, Window tinting, Air suspension
