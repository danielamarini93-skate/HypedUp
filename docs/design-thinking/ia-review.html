Step 5 · Content model

What every page contains

The rule book for writing. Every page: Hypa's voice, each fact once, "from £" only, one action.

21 pages · the front doors

Service page

  1. Hero: H1 in search words, 1–2 line answer, from £, Contact us, real photo
  2. Options: 2–4 choices (S5/S7, Stage 1–3, 1–3 channel…)
  3. How it works: 3 steps
  4. Proof: builds for this service, a review, partner logos
  5. Price detail: what changes the price, timings
  6. Good to know: legal note only where needed
  7. FAQ: 4–6 real search questions
  8. Your car (car-specific links) · Related services + guides · Contact band
3 pages · browse + SEO

Group hub

  1. Hero: what the group covers
  2. Every service in the group, same list and order as the footer, with from £
  3. Which one do I need? comparison
  4. Explainer (Security: S5 or S7? · Upgrades: will CarPlay fit my car? · Styling: wrap, PPF or ceramic?)
  5. Proof · FAQ · Contact band

Home

  1. Hero + video + Contact us
  2. The three groups
  3. Recent builds
  4. How a job works
  5. Reviews, Hypa, partners
  6. Visit · Contact band

Other templates

  • Car-specific: publish only with 2+ real jobs on that make
  • Builds: filter by group; build page when a job has 4+ photos
  • Prices: one table per group, from £ + link
  • About: Hypa's story, every claim confirmed
  • Blog: question title, answer in 2 lines, links its service
