Wrap, PPF or ceramic?

A wrap changes the colour. PPF protects the paint from chips. Ceramic makes it shine and easy to clean. Many cars get two of the three.

How long does a full wrap take?

At least a week. Every panel is cleaned, prepped and wrapped by hand, edges tucked, and the car is checked before it goes home.

Is window tint legal?

The front side windows must let through at least 70% of light and the windscreen 75%. Rear windows can go darker.

Can you wrap just part of the car?

Yes. Roofs, bonnets, mirrors and race stripes are popular.
